Before you join

A few useful answers.

What can I use today?

You can try this fictional example and request a beta invitation. The project workspace, client checkpoints and AI assistance are the intended product experience, not a live project service on this website.

Do I get access straight away?

No. This is a request for an invitation. We’ll contact you by email about availability and next steps. Signing up does not create a product account.

Who is ScopeKind for?

Our initial focus is freelance brand and graphic designers handling multiple projects and revision rounds. This is a focus we’re testing, not a limit on how many clients you should have. Adjacent disciplines can also register interest.

How will the AI assistance work?

AI assistance is planned for the initial workflow: group feedback, surface possible contradictions and suggest clarifying questions. Original feedback stays available. The designer edits or dismisses suggestions and chooses what to share. This demo uses prepared text, not live AI.

Does every new request become an extra charge?

No. You decide what belongs in the agreed scope. A visual preference is not approval of a fee or delivery date. Additional work needs a separate, explicit agreement.

Will clients need an account?

The intended experience is a simple checkpoint through a shared link, without a client account. We’ll validate that flow in the beta.

What happens after the free beta?

A paid version is planned. We’ll share the terms before you choose whether to continue. Requesting an invitation does not commit you to a purchase.

Beta privacy notice

ScopeKind stores your email, consent and submission date to handle your beta invitation request. If you choose to share them, we also store your discipline and the difficulty you describe.

These details are stored privately and used to assess beta fit and contact you about participation. They are not displayed on this website. Please do not include confidential client information.
